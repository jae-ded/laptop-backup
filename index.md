# laptop-backup-rclone

A small personal tool, not a product or a service.

It is a Google sign-in client for [rclone](https://rclone.org), a free and open-source program. One person uses it to keep encrypted backups of their own laptop in their own Google Drive.

- Only its owner uses it. It is not offered to anyone else.
- Files are encrypted on the laptop before they are uploaded.
- It can see and change only the files and folders it created itself. It cannot see anything else in the Drive.
