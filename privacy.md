# Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy covers laptop-backup-rclone, the Google sign-in client described on the [home page](./).

## Who uses it

Only the owner of this site, to back up their own laptop to their own Google Drive. It is not offered to the public, and no one else uses it.

## What it can access

It asks Google only for the `drive.file` permission. That lets it see, create, change and delete only the files and folders it created itself in the owner's Drive. It cannot see any other Drive files, or any email, contacts, calendar or other Google data.

## What it does with that access

It stores backup files in the owner's Drive and reads them back to check or restore the backup. The files are encrypted on the laptop before upload, so their contents cannot be read in Drive.

## Sharing

Nothing is sold, shared or sent to anyone else. The data goes only between the owner's laptop and the owner's Google Drive.

## Tracking

This site and the tool add no analytics, cookies, tracking or advertising of their own. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which has its own privacy statement.

## Withdrawing access

The owner can remove the tool's access at any time in their Google Account, under Security, "Your connections to third-party apps & services".
